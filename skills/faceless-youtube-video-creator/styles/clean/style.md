# Clean (default)

The default look: your channel's own palette, fonts and logo from `faceless-youtube.config.json` (`look`), on a light dotted ground. Ideas read as a numbered list; real artifacts carry the proof.

## Imagery source

- Real artifacts as `doc`, `table`, `fields` and `chat` slides: the sheet, the prompt, the file, the settings you actually used.
- Real screenshots as `image` slides (drop PNGs in the video's `assets/`; blur names, emails and customer data first).
- `scene` slides (the mascot, a person, a robot, flat backgrounds in your palette) for situations and analogies; generated illustrations, when on, in an image style built from your palette.
- Your logo (`look.logo`) in the corner and on the end slide; without one, the wordmark is the channel name.

## Look

Light ground from `look.colors.ground`, `main` for numbers and bold, `second` for text and the dark slides (catches, chapter cards), `accent` for highlights. Display and body fonts from `look.fonts` (Fraunces and Inter are bundled); JetBrains Mono for labels and figures. "Idea n of N" labels and a progress bar.

## Narration

The script playbook's default: hook, intro with the channel line, ideas as a numbered list (claim, why, proof, catch, bridge), subscribe at the mid hook, summary as a checklist. First person, plain, a bit wry.

## Avoid

Drawn or generated pictures of a real product screen, invented UI, real customer names, more than three slides of the same layout in a row.
